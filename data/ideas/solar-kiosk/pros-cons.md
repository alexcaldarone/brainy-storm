# Pros & Cons Identified from Body (Categorized & Sorted)

*Sorted by impact and risk.*



## Pros Voiced in Discussion

### General — Medium Impact

High placement flexibility by using solar power, potentially enabling off-grid operation in transit zones without direct grid access.

### General — Medium Impact

Captures high-intent foot traffic by solving urgent, time-sensitive repair needs for daily commuters.

### General — Medium Impact

Lower utility and operating costs over time through renewable on-site energy generation.

## Cons Voiced in Discussion

### General — Medium Risk

Power output limitations: Solar arrays of kiosk size may struggle to generate sufficient wattage for power-intensive diagnostic and repair tools or extended battery backup.

### General — Medium Risk

Space and siting conflicts: High-density commuter corridors often feature overhead coverage, tunnels, or high-rise shading that severely degrade solar performance.

### General — Medium Risk

Unit economics friction: High upfront capital expenditure for solar, storage, and repair equipment relative to the low margin of simple on-the-go repairs.
