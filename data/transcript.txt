=== Session: Agentic Brainstorming Architecture & Competitive Strategy ===

Marko (Founder): Alright team, here is the vision: during live brainstorming sessions, people talk naturally. We shouldn't stop to take notes or manually create slides. The engine transcribes our voices in real-time, segments dialogue into discrete idea cards stored in data/ideas.json, and continuously refines each idea's summary.
Alex (Lead Architect): Technically, we need a concrete architecture. The audio stream goes to a transcription worker, which emits context deltas to an Agentic Decision Router. The router asynchronously triggers specialized subagents (Workflow, Competitors, Pros/Cons), and updates are committed atomically to ideas.json while broadcasting WebSocket events. Can the workflow agent map out our pipeline architecture in Mermaid?
Sarah (Product Strategist): Let's check the competitive landscape. How do we compare against Miro, Whimsical, and Otter.ai? Teams already have Otter transcribing meetings and Miro for whiteboards. How do we position our voice-driven agentic tool calling against existing market players? Let's dispatch the competitor agent to search the web and define our positioning.
Elena (Security Engineer): I'm concerned about critical trade-offs. Recording raw unvetted brainstorming audio poses GDPR risks and proprietary secret leakage. Also, high-frequency LLM tool calls can cause token cost spikes. On the positive side, Marko's point about saving hours of manual meeting synthesis is massive. Let's analyze the pros, cons, and risks, and separate proponents from opponents.
Marko (Founder): Elena makes valid security points, but the velocity advantage is unmatched. Let's summarize all arguments, confirm Elena as an opponent on latency/privacy and Marko as a proponent on zero-friction ideation, and trigger a complete enrichment cycle across all subagents.
